### hi, i'm milo

i write and produce songs, and i build the tools i keep wishing i had while making them. most of what's here started as a problem in my own sessions: a tuner that was too slow, a sample folder i couldn't find anything in, a browser tab that ate an afternoon.

by day i work in neurotech at the UC Berkeley Neurotech Collider Lab.

#### audio plugins (JUCE, VST3 / AU)

- **[ripple](https://github.com/milomaurer23/ripple)**: an LFO modeled on a droplet hitting water, a damped oscillator instead of a sine
- **[tape](https://github.com/milomaurer23/tape)**: tape saturation built from scratch, with hysteresis, head bump, wow and flutter
- **[pulse](https://github.com/milomaurer23/pulse)**: sidechain-style ducking with tempo sync, kick-follow and MIDI triggers
- **[pebble](https://github.com/milomaurer23/pebble)**: a simple chromatic sampler instrument

#### iPhone

- **[Resonate](https://github.com/milomaurer23/Resonate)**: a chromatic guitar tuner with four swipeable views (gauge, dial, strobe, pitch trace)
- **[plumb](https://github.com/milomaurer23/plumb)**: a quieter, more minimal tuner. one needle, nothing else

#### focus + Mac

- **[Foqos Mac (Milo's personal build)](https://github.com/milomaurer23/foqos-for-mac)**: my personal Mac build of [Foqos](https://github.com/awaseem/foqos) by [Ali Waseem](https://github.com/awaseem). blocks sites and quits distracting apps during a session, with a focus tracker
- **[QuickNotes](https://github.com/milomaurer23/quicknotes-macos-widget)** and **[Timekeeper](https://github.com/milomaurer23/timekeeper-macos-widget)**: small SwiftUI widgets for daily notes and time tracking

#### music tools + experiments

- **[vibestack](https://github.com/milomaurer23/vibestack)**: a local-first loop and one-shot composer with real-time stretch and pitch
- **[music-sample-library-organizer](https://github.com/milomaurer23/music-sample-library-organizer)**: a Claude skill that reorganizes your sample library around how you actually produce
- **[minicraft-808](https://github.com/milomaurer23/minicraft-808)**: a tiny browser Minecraft with a TR-808 step sequencer built in, because why not

#### neurotech

- **[the-alzheimers-decoder](https://github.com/milomaurer23/the-alzheimers-decoder)**: a prototype timeline for logging a parent's day-to-day changes so the pattern is visible, not just felt
